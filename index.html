ABOUT RUULEMAX

Reliable Financial Consulting Firm Based in Liverpool UK

Founded and registered in Liverpool, England, RUULEMAX is a professional financial information consulting enterprise focusing on global capital market data analysis, financial planning consulting and asset allocation advice. We have gathered a group of senior financial analysts with rich cross-border market experience, each with an average of 15+ years in the financial industry.

Our core mission is to deliver accurate, real-time financial market information and customized consulting strategies to help our clients avoid market risks and capture reasonable investment returns in complex global financial environments. We adhere to the principle of "client-centric" and provide one-stop financial consulting services.

After years of steady development, our service network covers Europe, Southeast Asia and other global mainstream financial markets, winning long-term trust from thousands of individual investors and small and medium-sized financial institutions. Our compliance management system fully meets the regulatory requirements of the UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA).
